Shared things that pay for their own upkeep.

COMMONKIT gives every shared machine its own keyless NEAR account. Fees fund its upkeep, the machine opens its own maintenance tasks, and the people who keep it alive earn access that nobody can take back.

Sheet 02 · Money schematic

The machine keeps a small float. The rest flows on.

One reservation on testnet, traced through printer-01. The fee is revenue and fills the float. The deposit is members' money and sits in a separate vessel. When the float tops its cap, the overflow goes to the community's Sputnik DAO in the same callback.

FeeRevenue. Fills the float, pays upkeep.
DepositMembers' money. Hatched: a liability, refunded.
OverflowEverything above the cap leaves the machine.
