## Brand source and usage terms

- NEAR Foundation's public brand hub, "NEAR Narrative and Brand Hub"
  (https://near-foundation.notion.site/NEAR-Narrative-and-Brand-Hub-2f5da22d7b6480629d80ccf2b57fb4f9, read
  2026-11-13), describes itself as "the public source of truth for NEAR's narrative, messaging, and brand assets
  … for partners, ecosystem teams, media, and collaborators who want to communicate about NEAR". It offers
  `NEAR Token.zip` and `NEAR_Logos.zip`. The hub page did not render in our automated session, so the file here
  comes from NEAR's own site source instead; before submission, a person should download `NEAR Token.zip` from
  the hub and confirm it is the same mark (and replace this file with it if it differs).
- NEAR's earlier logo-usage page (pages.near.org, now redirected to docs.near.org) asked that the logo be used to
  refer to NEAR with generous clear space, and not as a personal or business avatar.
- The repository declares no licence: the mark is NEAR's trademark, all rights reserved.

## How COMMONKIT uses it

Nominative use only: a small glyph next to amounts denominated in NEAR (testnet), the way a currency symbol is
used, so a reader sees the unit at a glance. It is never our logo, never an avatar, never modified, recoloured,
outlined or animated, and never placed so it suggests endorsement by NEAR Foundation. Screen readers get the word
"NEAR" (the mark is `aria-hidden`), and running prose keeps the word.

### Social campaign cover (14 November 2026)

At the owner's request, the final COMMONKIT social cover also includes this exact, unmodified N mark next to the separate words “Built on NEAR”. It identifies the underlying network; COMMONKIT's own mark remains distinct and prominent. The NEAR mark is not used as the profile avatar or altered to create a joint logo.
