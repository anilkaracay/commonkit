## Scene generation

Use case: ads-marketing / premium industrial product still life. Create an ultra-wide 3:1 social profile cover background for COMMONKIT, a community-owned shared-machine upkeep product. Extremely polished art direction, an actual designed editorial product campaign, not generic AI blockchain imagery. Canvas composition: quiet pale cool-white negative space on the LEFT 42 percent for later typesetting, all devices in the RIGHT 58 percent. Four plausible, beautifully modelled real-world shared devices form a coherent compact workshop still life: an open-frame desktop FDM 3D printer as the hero (correct extruded aluminium frame, upright gantry, linear rails, one extrusion head over a print bed, one small cobalt blue printed object), a closed compact laser cutter with a smoky transparent lid low in the foreground, a front-loading shared washing machine with one glass circular door, and a modest tall community refrigerator behind. Vary heights and overlap naturally while keeping every device legible. Real manufactured proportions, restrained physical details, no brand names or nonsense controls. One continuous warm-grey studio floor and seamless cool-white backdrop. Satin ivory and graphite materials with selective rich cobalt #2453e0 painted panels, tiny restrained warm orange functional detail on the printer bed. Precise soft contact shadows, rich yet soft directional daylight from upper left, subtle warm bounce, gentle occlusion, elegant industrial-design catalogue photography, physically believable reflections, exceptionally crisp high-resolution fine material detail. Camera is a slightly elevated three-quarter product view, moderately long lens, straight verticals, NOT isometric cartoon, no dramatic wide-angle distortion. Objects are grounded together rather than floating on platforms. White space at upper-left and lower-left stays genuinely uncluttered; bottom-left must stay empty for a circular profile avatar overlap. Keep critical objects inside central 80 percent vertical height for responsive cropping. No text, no letters, no watermarks, no logos, no UI, no cables weaving around, no glowing networks, no coins, no space scenery, no gradients used as decorative blobs, no neon, no plastic toy look. Overall feeling: restrained, expensive, engineered and tangible.
